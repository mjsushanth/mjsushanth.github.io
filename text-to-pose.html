Text-to-Pose: CLIP-Conditioned Diffusion for 3D Human Pose

A CLIP-conditioned diffusion model that turns captions into 3D human skeletons on HumanML3D, paired with retrieval evaluators and conditioning analysis to test how well it works. Built in PyTorch around a 22-joint, 66-dimensional pose representation.
  • Data and Task Design: Reduced HumanML3D motion to 66-D static poses and used k-means with balanced sampling across 8 clusters, since one cluster held 49.6% of the data. Captions were cut to the first action using POS tags.
  • Cross-Modal Architecture: Built a hybrid CNN-Transformer UNet that projects frozen CLIP ViT-B/32 embeddings from 512D to 256D and injects them by cross-attention at several scales: coarse layers for global posture, fine layers for limb placement.
  • Training and Guidance: Trained a 1000-step DDPM with a squared-cosine noise schedule, mixed precision and OneCycleLR, using dual-pass classifier-free guidance ramped from 2.0 to 7.0. Ran ablations over guidance strength and conditioning dropout, and cut training time per step by 9.9x.
  • Anatomical Correctness: Added bone-length and pelvis-spine-extremity chain losses in denormalized metric space, reaching a final anatomy loss of 0.07. Also traced a pose-decoding bug to the wrong 66 of 263 per-frame features; fixing it restored constant bone lengths.
  • Evaluation and Results: Built a text-motion retrieval evaluator that reproduces the published R-Precision top-3 of 0.797 on real motions, confirmed by a second independent evaluator. Scored with it, a 17.9M-parameter MDM trained for 3,000 steps reaches 3.17x chance on held-out captions. Also built pose visualizers and an interactive caption-retrieval demo.
  • Conditioning Analysis: Found that CLIP separates left from right poorly, yet 4,640 test captions show no spatial gap. Truncating captions to the first action dropped R-Precision from 0.8013 to 0.6563.

technical details

  • Spatial probe: caption pairs differing only by left vs right embed closer in CLIP than pairs differing by a comparable non-spatial word (Cohen's d 0.995, n = 40 per group), and 57.6% of 24,503 captions use spatial words
  • Evaluator check: reproduces the published R-Precision top-3 of 0.797 on real motions across five full-split runs
  • Seed noise: the control arm scored 0.2969 and 0.3438 R-Precision across two seeds, the same size as the arm-to-arm gap, and FID swung from 7.209 to 11.044 within one arm
  • Detection bound: separating the truncation effect from noise would need about 1,780 generated samples per arm per seed
