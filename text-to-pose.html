Text-to-Pose: CLIP-Conditioned Diffusion for 3D Human Pose

Text-to-pose generation on HumanML3D: a CLIP-conditioned diffusion model that turns captions into 3D human skeletons, paired with a rigorous evaluation layer of retrieval evaluators and conditioning analysis. Built in PyTorch around a 22-joint, 66-dimensional pose representation.
  • Data and Task Design: HumanML3D motion reduced to 66-D static poses, k-means clustering with 8-cluster balanced sampling to avoid 49.6% cluster dominance, and captions cut to the first action with POS tags
  • Cross-Modal Architecture: Hybrid CNN–Transformer UNet where frozen CLIP ViT-B/32 embeddings are projected 512D to 256D and injected by cross-attention at several scales, coarse layers for global posture and fine layers for limb placement
  • Training and Guidance: 1000-step DDPM with squared-cosine noise schedule, mixed precision and OneCycleLR, and dual-pass classifier-free guidance ramped from 2.0 to 7.0, with ablations over guidance strength and conditioning dropout
  • Anatomical Constraints: Bone-length consistency and pelvis–spine–extremity kinematic chains enforced as auxiliary anatomical losses, computed in denormalized metric space, with a final anatomy loss of 0.07
  • Pose Decoding: Traced a pose-decode bug to the wrong 66 of 263 features per frame and fixed it, restoring constant bone lengths across frames
  • Evaluation Harness: Text-motion retrieval evaluator reproduces the published R-Precision top-3 of 0.797 on real motions across five full-split runs, cross-checked by a second independent evaluator
  • Conditioning Findings: CLIP separates left from right poorly (Cohen's d 0.995, 57.6% of captions spatial) yet 4,640 test captions show no spatial gap; truncating captions cuts R-Precision from 0.8013 to 0.6563
  • Small-Budget Results: A 17.9M-parameter MDM trained for 3,000 steps scores R-Precision top-3 of 0.2969, 3.17x chance on held-out captions it never trained on
  • Engineering: Training step cut from 2.284 to 0.231 s/step (9.9x), with EDA and joint-mapping tools, pose visualizers, and an interactive caption-retrieval demo at about 10s per motion
