Multi-View 3D Scene Analysis on ETH3D

10k+ LOC pipeline for pose-guided multi-view feature correspondence on the ETH3D dataset (scene, feature-track, and SfM analysis), built to find reliable matches across cluttered scenes and varying baselines.
  • Pose-Guided Correspondence: Camera poses and view-overlap analysis select candidate view pairs before matching. SIFT features (1.0M+ across 25.7MP views) are matched with a FLANN KD-tree and Lowe ratio test.
  • Validation and Multi-View Tracks: Matches are verified with RANSAC and epipolar constraints, rejecting unstable matches and low-baseline pairs. Survivors are promoted into 438 multi-view tracks using match-quality scoring and occlusion-aware masking.
  • COLMAP Integration: Custom keypoints, descriptors and two-view geometry were written into a COLMAP database, so its incremental mapper consumed these correspondences without re-extraction.
  • Preprocessing and Reproducibility: GPU preprocessing (PyTorch LAB color transforms, CLAHE, bilateral filtering) yields 56K+ features per image on representative scenes. HDF5 and JSON artifacts per scene capture intrinsics, poses, feature maps and match statistics for reproducible runs.
  • Scope and Depth: The work goes deep on correspondence, feature-track and SfM-readiness analysis, and runs through to COLMAP's mapper rather than a full reconstruction.
