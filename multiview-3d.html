Multi-View 3D Scene Analysis on ETH3D

10k+ LOC pipeline for pose-guided multi-view feature correspondence on the ETH3D dataset (scene, feature-track, and SfM analysis). Integrates camera pose estimation, feature matching, occlusion handling, and multi-view consistency checks to build robust correspondences across cluttered scenes and varying baselines, with explicit tooling for diagnostics and reproducible experiments.
  • Pose-Guided Correspondence: Camera poses and view-overlap analysis gate candidate view pairs before matching; 1.0M+ SIFT features across 25.7MP views, matched with a FLANN KD-tree and Lowe ratio test
  • Geometric Validation: RANSAC fundamental-matrix verification with epipolar constraints, inlier-ratio analysis, and rejection stages for unstable matches and low-baseline pairs prone to degeneracies
  • Multi-View Tracks & Occlusion Handling: Matches promoted into 438 multi-view tracks that stay consistent across several cameras, using match-quality scoring, track-formation logic, and occlusion-aware masking
  • COLMAP Integration: Custom keypoints, descriptors, and two-view geometry written into a COLMAP database, so its incremental mapper consumed these correspondences without re-extraction
  • GPU-Accelerated Preprocessing: PyTorch LAB color transforms, CLAHE, and bilateral filtering on the GPU; reaches 56K+ features per image with ~28% spatial coverage and a 0.93 match-quality score on representative ETH3D scenes
  • Experiment Infrastructure: HDF5 and JSON artifact versioning per scene, capturing camera intrinsics, poses, feature maps, and match statistics for reproducible runs and ablations
  • Scope and Depth: An intensive build that went deep on correspondence, feature-track, and SfM-readiness analysis, carrying the pipeline through to COLMAP's mapper rather than a complete reconstruction
