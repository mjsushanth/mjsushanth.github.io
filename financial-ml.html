May 2022 – November 2023

  • Attrition Hazard Model: Modeled assignment attrition as a discrete-time XGBoost hazard model, improving two-month AUC from 0.56 to 0.71 for redeployment planning

  • Rate and Margin Guidance: Developed an XGBoost quantile regression for rate and margin guidance, cutting bill-rate error from $6.20/hr to $3.65/hr at 81.2% coverage against an 80% prediction-interval target

  • Cash-Timing Hazard Model: Built a cash-timing hazard model on the same monthly AR snapshots, reaching 0.7958 AUC for invoice payment timing
  • Anomaly Detection: Developed anomaly detection on expenditure and assignment data using deterministic controls, Isolation Forest and autoencoders, surfacing 45,685 lines representing $37.17M in revenue against near-zero pay
