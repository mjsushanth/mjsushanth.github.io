Vision-Language Late-Interaction Retrieval with LoRA

A ColBERT-style late-interaction retrieval system that pairs ViT patch tokens with CLIP text tokens through a learned projection, fine-tuned with LoRA on 1,500 Flickr8k image-caption pairs. Query tokens are scored against image patches with MaxSim and trained with a symmetric InfoNCE loss.
  • Multi-Vector Architecture: Pairs 196 ViT-base/patch16 patch tokens per image with per-token CLIP text embeddings through a learned 512-d to 768-d projection. MaxSim scores each query token against its best-matching patch, tying words to image regions.
  • Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning: LoRA adapters on the ViT attention layers train only 1.3M of 87M parameters, keeping the pretrained spatial understanding while shifting patch features toward the CLIP text space.
  • Contrastive Training: Trained with symmetric InfoNCE over the full batch-by-batch similarity matrix, cutting the loss by 38% (0.858 to 0.534).
  • Interpretability: Token-to-patch MaxSim heatmaps show where each word looks, such as "dog" lighting up dog regions and "sunlight" bright areas. t-SNE of the pooled embeddings shows distinct image and text clusters.

technical details

  • Images: 224x224 input split into 196 patch tokens of 768-d (ViT-base/patch16)
  • Projection: CLIP text space 512-d mapped to ViT space 768-d
  • LoRA: rank 16, alpha 32, dropout 0.05 on ViT qkv and proj modules, 1.3M of 87M parameters trainable (1.49%)
  • Training: 30 epochs, AdamW (lr 3e-5, weight decay 1e-2), ReduceLROnPlateau (patience 3), gradient clipping at 1.0, early stopping (patience 6)
  • Loss: symmetric InfoNCE 0.858 to 0.534, against a random baseline of log(8) ≈ 2.08
  • Score separation on 8-sample test batches: matched pairs 0.20 to 0.27, mismatches below 0.15
