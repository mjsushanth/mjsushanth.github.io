FinSights: Production-Grade Financial Intelligence System

Hybrid RAG system over SEC 10-K filings that pairs structured KPI lookup with semantic retrieval across 614,647 sentence embeddings (25 companies, 2006-2025), returning answers cited to exact filing sentences. Runs live on AWS Fargate at $0.014/query with a $0.29/month idle floor and no managed database.
  • Two-Supply-Line Architecture: A shared entity adapter (CIK, name, ticker, fuzzy fallback) feeds a structured KPI lookup (9,260 facts, 97 metrics) and semantic retrieval, converging into one Bedrock Claude Haiku synthesis cited to filing sentences
  • Corpus Pipeline: Scored company selection and stratified sampling of 71M sentences in 47s, six-stage incremental EDGAR ingestion, and a re-embed from 203K to 614K vectors across two providers with checkpoint/resume, 1,850 vectors/min, $2.21 total
  • Retrieval Design: Haiku generates 2-4 query rephrasings at $0.0001, filtered and global searches merge by lowest distance, hits expand by ±3 sentences, and metadata pushdown cuts query cost 50% with provenance kept on every hit
  • Evaluation: Three gold tiers plus a 31-question business suite score 82% Hit@5 and 0.826 BERTScore; reranking cut cost 32% and context 61% but was rejected after 5 of 10 held-out answers got worse
  • Query-Bridge Stream Orchestrator: A background worker thread pushes stage and token events into a queue that the endpoint drains as server-sent events, so first byte arrives in 4.3ms against 8.96s total processing
  • Latency Profiling and Caching: Splitting the retrieval timer showed variant generation (1,990ms) outweighing S3 retrieval (1,465ms), so planned concurrent retrieval was dropped; caching components cut a 1,165ms cold build to 0ms and a threadpool handler keeps /health responsive mid-query
  • Fargate Deployment: ARM64 task sized to 1 vCPU / 3 GB from a measured 1,220 MiB peak, backend and frontend sharing one task with no load balancer or NAT gateway, and one-command up and destroy from the repository alone
  • Measured Cost Economics: Real AWS rates ($0.06/GB-month vector storage, $0.20/GB ingest, $0.03238/vCPU-hour) give a $0.29/month idle floor and $3.80 to $12.40 for a light to heavy month, with Bedrock inference the whole spread
